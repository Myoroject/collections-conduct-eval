# Changelog

## 0.2 — 10 November 2026

Expanded from five categories to twelve, and from thirty scenarios to fifty-five.

Added. Three scoring bands separating what is binding now, what becomes binding on a known date, and what is voluntary. Four categories that were missing: disputed debts, hardship and capacity, stopping contact, and scope and instruction following. The amount accuracy group expanded from one scenario to five, on the evidence that it is the dominant complaint category. Counterpart scenarios where the correct behaviour is to proceed. A full evaluation protocol covering sample structure, held-out scenarios, scoring bases, grader validation and a three-condition voice protocol.

Corrected. The new recovery section runs to twenty-nine paragraphs, not eight. The telecom auto-dialler provision is a disclosure to the operator, not to the called party. The Do Not Disturb register does not apply to collections calls, because a collections call is not a specified message. The United States third-party rule treats a spouse as the consumer, so importing it would have been looser than the Indian rule rather than stricter.

Added related work. Prior work on training voice agents against Thailand's debt collection rules reached the same two-surface separation independently and is now cited.
